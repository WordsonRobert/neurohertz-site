    01 · The signal

    Nineteen waves, five hundred times a second

    Every electrode reports a voltage every two milliseconds. Ten minutes of eyes-closed rest becomes a matrix: one row per electrode, one column per instant.

    X ∈ ℝ19 × N N = 600 s × 500 Hz = 300,000

    Rows
    19 electrodes
    Columns
    300,000 instants
    Per recording
    5.7 M numbers

    Each electrode hears a weighted mix of sources through skull and scalp. P3 is a place on the head, not a node in the brain.

    02 · Clean

    Take out everything that isn't brain

    Mains hum, slow drift, blinks and electrode pops ride on top of the signal. Each of the 19 rows is cleaned on its own.

    • Band-pass 0.5–80 Hz — wide enough to keep the whole gamma band. The dataset's published derivatives stop at 45 Hz, so we start from raw.
    • Notch at 50 Hz for mains hum.
    • Average reference — subtract the 19-electrode mean at every instant, so each column sums to zero.
    • Spike repair — flag samples beyond five robust deviations and redraw them from their neighbours.
    How the spike rule decides

    x = [2, 3, 4, 3, 2, 50, 3, 2, 1, 3]

    1. median = 3, so |x − 3| = [1, 0, 1, 0, 1, 47, 0, 1, 2, 0]
    2. MAD = median of that = 1. The 47 barely moves it.
    3. σ̂ = MAD / 0.6745 ≈ 1.48, a standard deviation the spike can't inflate
    4. threshold = 5σ̂ ≈ 7.41, and |50| > 7.41, so it's flagged
    5. ±10 samples (±20 ms) around it are redrawn by linear interpolation

    N samples in, N samples out. The recording is repaired, never shortened.

    03 · Decompose

    One wave becomes eight

    Variational Mode Decomposition splits one electrode's wave into K = 8 narrow-band waves that add back up to the original. Each carries a centre frequency ω. We keep the mode nearest 40 Hz and set the other seven aside.

    x(t) ≈ u1(t) + u2(t) + … + u8(t)

    ω on this recording: computing…

    Read at P3, over the left parietal scalp — the channel used for 82 of 88 recordings.

    04 · Rhythm

    Ask the gamma wave what time it is

    The Hilbert transform turns the gamma wave into a rotating phasor with an amplitude A(t) and a phase φ(t). How fast the phase turns is the instantaneous frequency: a fresh reading every 2 ms, not one number for ten minutes.

    z(t) = A(t) · eiφ(t) f(t) = Δφ ⁄ 2π × 500 Hz

    Envelope
    how strong gamma is
    Phase
    where in its cycle it is
    Frequency
    how fast it's cycling, now

    05 · The biomarker

    One number: how much the beat wanders

    Keep the readings between 25 and 80 Hz and take their standard deviation. A steady rhythm scores low and a wandering one scores high. That one number, std_ifreq, is the entire biomarker.

    Healthy controls · n = 295.53 ± 1.09 Hz
    Alzheimer's · n = 367.17 ± 1.89 Hz

    Mann–Whitney p < 0.0001. Within the Alzheimer's group, higher instability goes with lower MMSE (Spearman r = −0.43, p = 0.009). On screen are two synthetic 4-second recordings measured live; clinical values come from ten-minute recordings and run higher.

    06 · The headset

    And this is what was listening

    Nineteen dry electrodes in the clinical 10–20 layout, the same montage the pipeline was validated on, wired into three ADC zones and one pod at the back of the head. No gel and no scanner: ten minutes in a chair.

    Channels
    19
    Layout
    10–20 system
    Contacts
    Dry electrodes
    Session
    10 min, eyes closed
    Pipeline rate
    500 Hz

    Three ADC zones

    Left, right and midline, each on its own converter

    • ADC-L Fp1 · F7 · F3 · T3 · C3 · T5 · P3 · O1
    • ADC-R Fp2 · F8 · F4 · T4 · C4 · T6 · P4 · O2
    • ADC-M Fz · Cz · Pz and the main pod

    Serpentine links run between neighbouring electrodes. The zig-zag gives every contact room to settle against the scalp.

    The pod

    Conversion, control and audio at the back of the head

    The main pod carries the midline converter, the microcontroller and the audio driver. Acoustic tubes run from it to both ears, and the left and right hubs report to it over a shared bus.

    • SPI bus hub-to-pod data
    • Acoustic tubes pod to both ears
    • Mechanical links structure only

    The montage

    Odd numbers left, even numbers right, z on the midline

    Hover or tap an electrode to see the region it sits over.

    07 · Tuned per recording

    Every recording gets its own decomposition

    One setting of K and α doesn't fit every head. For each recording we run 16 decompositions on the parietal channel and a judge picks one: a variational autoencoder trained on 1.39 million half-second gamma segments from healthy controls. The decomposition whose gamma it reconstructs best is kept.

    11 · Where it fits
    
      
    Ten minutes, a chair and a headset
    
      
    EEG is cheap, quiet and already in most hospitals. A dry headset and a single readable number take it out of the specialist lab.
    
    
    
    
    
      
    
        
        
    Memory clinics
    
        
    An objective physiological measure to sit beside cognitive testing, recorded in the same visit.
    
      
    
      
    
        
        
    Primary & community care
    
        
    No scanner, no gel, no specialist on site. Built toward deciding who should be referred, and sooner.
    
      
    
      
    
        
        
    Trials & cohorts
    
        
    One interpretable number to track across visits, with every processing step logged and reproducible.
    
      
    
      
    
        
        
    Open research
    
        
    Trained and tested on open data, with code that runs end to end. Anyone can check the number.
    
      
    
    
    
  
    

    



    
  
    
    
    
      
    12 · Evidence
    
      
    What the numbers say, and what they don't
    
    
    
    
    
      
    
        
    ✓ What we claim
    
        
      
          
    • std_ifreq at P3 separates Alzheimer's from controls on ds004504 (p < 0.0001).
      • 
          
    • Within Alzheimer's, it tracks cognitive severity (MMSE r = −0.43, p = 0.009).
      • 
          
    • It holds up on held-out subjects: 73.8% accuracy, AUC 0.781 under leave-one-subject-out.
      • 
          
    • It does this with one feature where comparable models use 95.
      • 
        
    
      
    
      
    
        
    ✕ What we don't, yet
    
        
      
          
    • Early detection. These patients were already diagnosed; the dataset has no prodromal or MCI group.
      • 
          
    • Generalisation. This is one dataset, one site and 65 subjects, not yet tested on an independent cohort.
      • 
          
    • State-of-the-art accuracy. Multi-feature models score higher.
      • 
          
    • Clinical readiness. NeuroHertz is a research prototype, not a medical device.
      • 
        
    
      
    
      
    
        
    → What comes next
